Why is every tier a flat fee?

Because audits run on your own provider keys. The subscription prices the platform — projects, monitored URLs, history retention, support — not per-audit usage, so a heavier audit month never produces a surprise invoice.

What does model usage cost?

Model spend is billed by your provider at their rates, directly to your account — OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic. Searchify never marks it up, and your keys are encrypted at rest and never returned by the API.

Can I try before I pay?

The Free tier runs forever — a real site-health sample crawl with scores and remediation guidance. Paid tiers start with a free trial, and Enterprise includes a security review before you sign anything.
