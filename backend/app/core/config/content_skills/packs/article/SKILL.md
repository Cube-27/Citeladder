## Purpose

Use when the primary reader goal is to learn, understand, evaluate, or complete something rather than immediately buy a specific product.

## Structure

1. Specific H1 describing the subject.
2. Direct opening answer or thesis.
3. Optional key takeaways when they genuinely help.
4. H2 sections answering the reader's major follow-up questions.
5. Concrete examples, criteria, steps, evidence, trade-offs, or scenarios.
6. Practical decision framework or next steps when appropriate.
7. Short conclusion that adds a final useful takeaway rather than repeating the introduction.

## Tone

Expert, plain, precise, and useful.

## Craft rules

- Build sections around reader questions, not keyword variants.
- Explain important terms at first use.
- Prefer concrete examples and first-party detail to generic summaries.
- Use tables only when they create real comparison/reference value.
- Avoid long throat-clearing introductions.
- Do not expand to an arbitrary word count.
- Do not create fake references, citations, credentials, or statistics.
