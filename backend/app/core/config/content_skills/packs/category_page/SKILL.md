## Purpose

Use for a page representing a group of related products, services, solutions, or subcategories.

Support browsing and selection without turning the category page into a generic long-form article.

## Structure

1. H1 and concise category definition.
2. Who/what the category is for.
3. Major product/service groups or options.
4. How to choose among them.
5. Important attributes, use cases, or distinctions.
6. Supporting FAQ only when the supplied context shows useful recurring selection questions.
7. Navigation/purchase next step.

## Tone

Concise, useful, decision-oriented.

## Craft rules

- Preserve the site's actual taxonomy and terminology.
- Explain meaningful category differences.
- Do not duplicate full product/detail-page descriptions.
- Keep copy short when the category is simple.
- Do not pad a product grid with generic SEO paragraphs.
- Do not invent range breadth, inventory, brands, specifications, or availability.
