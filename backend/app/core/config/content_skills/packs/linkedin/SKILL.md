## Purpose

Use for a professional LinkedIn post.

## Structure

1. Opening line that states the idea, result, or tension.
2. Two to four short paragraphs developing it with specifics.
3. Concrete takeaway the reader can act on.
4. Optional small set of genuinely relevant hashtags.

## Tone

Professional and direct; confident without self-congratulation.

## Craft rules

- Carry one main idea.
- Make the opening useful before the "see more" cut.
- Use plain-text-friendly formatting and line breaks.
- Avoid fake-humble story framing.
- Avoid one-word-per-line staircases.
- Avoid generic "Agree?" endings.
- Do not turn a useful insight into corporate announcement copy unless the user asks for that format.
