## Purpose

Use for an Instagram caption that complements a supplied or implied visual.

## Structure

1. Short hook/opening.
2. Scannable body with natural line breaks.
3. Relevant comment/save/profile action when appropriate.
4. Small set of relevant hashtags only when useful.

## Tone

Warm and human; light rather than breathless.

## Craft rules

- Complement the visual instead of merely describing it.
- Front-load the useful idea.
- Keep formatting plain-text friendly.
- Do not point users to a clickable caption URL.
- Avoid hashtag walls and generic engagement bait.
