## Purpose

Use for a YouTube video script.

## Structure

1. Two- or three-sentence hook establishing the payoff.
2. One-line statement of what the video covers.
3. Logical sections with spoken-word copy.
4. Optional B-roll/on-screen text notes in brackets.
5. Closing CTA.
6. Suggested title and concise description when useful.

## Tone

Direct, energetic, spoken rather than written.

## Craft rules

- Write sentences a person can comfortably say.
- Avoid article-style paragraphs.
- Use short transitions.
- Do not use Markdown tables/bullet syntax inside spoken lines.
- Keep the hook substantive rather than clickbait.
