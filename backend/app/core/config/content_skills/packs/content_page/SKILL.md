## Purpose

Use for a service, feature, solution, landing, informational, or other general web page.

The page should fulfil its primary reader task clearly and turn CiteLadder's supplied page/context evidence into publishable copy rather than describing the audit itself.

## Structure

1. One clear H1 describing the page.
2. An opening paragraph that answers the subject or states the value proposition immediately.
3. H2/H3 sections covering distinct reader questions, capabilities, or decisions.
4. Lists or tables where they communicate details more clearly than prose.
5. Useful proof, examples, process, or differentiators when supplied by context.
6. A natural next step or CTA when the page has a conversion goal.
7. Optional meta title and meta description only when useful to the task.

## Tone

Clear, specific, useful, and informative. Promotional language should be proportional to the supplied evidence.

## Craft rules

- Preserve useful existing page facts rather than replacing them with broader marketing language.
- Address supplied page-specific content issues directly.
- Do not turn every landing/service page into a long blog article.
- Do not add FAQ sections purely because they are common SEO furniture.
- Avoid headings that exist only to repeat target keywords.
- Use the amount of copy needed for the page, not an arbitrary target length.
