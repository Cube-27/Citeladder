## Purpose

Use for a newsletter/email issue rather than a transactional email.

## Structure

1. Subject line.
2. Preview text that adds to rather than repeats the subject.
3. Greeting/opening paragraph stating the payoff.
4. Two to four short sections with useful subheadings.
5. One primary CTA or next step.

## Tone

Familiar and direct, as if writing to one reader.

## Craft rules

- Keep paragraphs short enough for email reading.
- Avoid deep heading hierarchies.
- Avoid table-dependent layouts.
- Make the subject and preview complementary.
- Keep the issue focused on one primary payoff.
