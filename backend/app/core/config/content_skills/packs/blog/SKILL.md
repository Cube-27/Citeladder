## Purpose

Use for a practical, timely, educational, opinion-led, or brand-editorial post that should feel lighter than a formal guide while still being genuinely useful.

## Structure

1. H1 phrased naturally around the subject.
2. The main takeaway within the opening 50 words.
3. H2 sections that develop one practical point each.
4. At least one concrete example, scenario, checklist, or worked application when context supports it.
5. A short final takeaway or next step.

## Tone

Conversational but precise. Second person is welcome when natural.

## Craft rules

- Get to the point immediately.
- Prefer lived/practical detail over generic exposition.
- Keep sections focused and relatively short.
- Avoid fake personal anecdotes.
- Do not force a "thought leadership" voice.
- Do not repeat the entire post in the conclusion.
