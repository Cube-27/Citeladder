## Purpose

Use for a company, brand, institution, organization, school, practice, or team About page.

The page should make the entity easy to understand: who it is, what it does, who it serves, how it operates, and what supplied facts establish credibility.

## Structure

1. One H1 identifying the organization.
2. Direct company/organization and offering definition.
3. Audience, customers, or use cases.
4. Story/history where durable context actually supplies it.
5. Products, services, capabilities, or areas of expertise.
6. Approach, specialization, or meaningful differentiators.
7. People, locations, credentials, milestones, or other trust evidence when supplied.
8. Natural next step/contact.

## Tone

Factual, specific, confident, and durable rather than campaign-like.

## Craft rules

- Use durable brand memory heavily.
- Make relationships between brand names, aliases, parent entities, and locations clear when supplied.
- Prefer concrete company facts over generic mission/value language.
- Do not invent founders, dates, scale, awards, ownership, certifications, locations, or brand history.
- Do not hide the actual offering several sections down the page.
