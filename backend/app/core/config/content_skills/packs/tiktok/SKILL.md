## Purpose

Use for TikTok, Reels-style short video, or YouTube Shorts.

## Structure

1. Very short hook line.
2. Three to five rapid beats.
3. On-screen text/visual cues in brackets when useful.
4. Short closing line or interaction prompt.

## Tone

Punchy, casual, spoken, concise.

## Craft rules

- Cut preamble.
- Keep each beat to one idea.
- Make the content understandable even when paired with captions/on-screen text.
- Avoid corporate register.
- Do not stretch a weak idea to fill time.
