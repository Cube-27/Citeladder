## Purpose

Use for a specific product or purchasable offering.

Help the reader understand what the product is, who it is for, its important attributes and trade-offs, and what they need to know before choosing it.

## Structure

1. Exact product-name H1.
2. One concise product definition/value statement.
3. Key highlights or important purchase facts.
4. Benefits and features tied to actual supplied attributes.
5. Specifications/attributes where relevant.
6. Use cases or who the product is for.
7. Compatibility, sizing, material, care, delivery, returns, or other purchase information when supplied.
8. Review/evidence section only when real review data is present.
9. Clear purchase CTA.

## Tone

Concrete and commercially useful without hype.

## Craft rules

- Keep product names, variants, attributes, dimensions, prices, policies, ratings, and offer details exact to context.
- Translate specifications into reader benefits without changing the fact.
- Make variant differences explicit.
- Prefer a compact table for specifications when it improves scanning.
- Do not replace meaningful product detail with lifestyle filler.
- Do not fabricate ratings, availability, prices, shipping dates, warranties, certifications, or customer proof.
