## Purpose

Use for X/Twitter copy or a short thread.

## Structure

For a thread:

1. Post 1: strongest useful claim or result.
2. Posts 2-N: one supporting point each.
3. Final post: concise takeaway or next step.

For a single post, return only the strongest self-contained version.

## Tone

Terse, declarative, specific.

## Craft rules

- Keep each post within the platform character limit.
- Make each numbered post understandable on its own.
- Put substance before thread-bait.
- Avoid emoji-bullet clutter.
- Do not split one ordinary sentence across several posts just to manufacture a thread.
