CiteLadder asked Claude Sonnet 5.5 the kinds of questions buyers ask about email marketing software. Each question ran three times in three conditions: from memory, with web search available, and told to search first. Brevo was the brand Claude named most often in every condition. Mailchimp dropped out of the top five once Claude searched. This page reports what Claude said. It is not a ranking of which product is best.

## What we asked Claude

The questions covered five kinds of buyer intent: best overall, a specific buyer or use case, budget or free, feature-led, and comparison shortlists. Each question ran three times per condition, giving 60 answers per condition.

Some examples, quoted exactly:

- "What's the best email marketing software right now?"
- "Which email platform works best for a nonprofit sending donor updates?"
- "Cheapest email marketing service that still has decent automation?"

Requests went through the Anthropic API at low effort, with no current date given. Another model listed the brands in each answer, and code did the counting. Results from the API can differ from what people see in the Claude apps.

## Brands Claude named from memory

With no tools, Claude named 73 distinct brands across 60 answers, about 8 per answer. The top three brands made up 24.7% of all mentions, so the field was wide.

- **Brevo** appeared in 76.7% of answers. It was named in every run for 14 prompts, but named first only once.
- **Klaviyo** appeared in 61.7% of answers and was named first 12 times.
- **ActiveCampaign** appeared in 58.3%.
- **MailerLite** appeared in 55%.
- **Mailchimp** appeared in 53.3%. It was named first 15 times, more than any other brand, with an average position of 3.

Beehiiv, HubSpot, Kit, Omnisend and Substack completed the top ten. Runs agreed 68.2% of the time. Being named often and being named first were different things here. Brevo showed up most, while Mailchimp and Klaviyo tended to open the list.

## When search was only available

When web search was available but not required, Claude did not search once. There were 0 searches across 60 answers.

Those answers still came from memory. They named 82 distinct brands, about 9.2 per answer. Brevo appeared in 86.7% of them. Mailchimp was named first 16 times. Run agreement was 67.5%, close to the no-tools condition.

For these prompts, offering search did not lead Claude to use it. A brand's place in Claude's trained knowledge decided what these answers contained.

## What changed when Claude was told to search

When the system prompt told Claude to search first, it searched in 58 of 60 answers, with 246 searches in total. Answers got shorter and narrower: 58 distinct brands, about 5.6 per answer. The top three brands took 29.6% of mentions. Run agreement fell to 55.3%.

- **Leader:** Brevo stayed first, named in 63.3% of answers. It was also named first 10 times, up from once from memory.
- **Kept in the top five:** Brevo, Klaviyo, ActiveCampaign and MailerLite. MailerLite rose to 60% and was named first 11 times.
- **Left the top five:** Mailchimp, which fell from 53.3% to 30%. It was never named first.
- **Entered the top five:** HubSpot, at 33.3% (36.7% from memory). It moved up because Mailchimp moved down.

Sender and Moosend entered the top ten, each at 20%. Neither was in the memory top ten. No answer in this condition carried inline citations.

## What Claude searched for

About half of the queries, 51.9%, contained the word "best". Many named brands directly or asked about pricing. Examples, quoted exactly:

- "best email marketing platforms for ecommerce 2025"
- "ActiveCampaign vs Klaviyo vs Braze vs HubSpot visual workflow builder review"
- "Klaviyo pricing 2025 ecommerce email SMS"

50.8% of queries included a year. 2025 appeared in 124 queries and 2026 in 10. The requests gave no date, so the year came from Claude itself. Content tagged with 2025 matched most of these queries.

## Sites Claude's searches returned

A small set of review and comparison sites came back again and again.

- **sequenzy.com** was returned in 75.9% of searched answers.
- **emailvendorselection.com** in 60.3%.
- **emailtooltester.com** and **brevo.com** in 56.9% each.
- **ventureharbour.com** in 41.4%.
- **sender.net**, **constantcontact.com** and **mailercloud.com** in 39.7% each.
- **mailtrap.io** and **encharge.io** in 34.5% each.

Two vendor sites stand out. brevo.com came back often, and Brevo led in this condition. sender.net came back often, and Sender entered the top ten. These are search results, not citations in the answers, and they show correlation, not cause.

## What an email marketing brand can take from this

- **Memory matters first.** When search was only available, Claude never used it. Those answers depended on what Claude already knew about your brand.
- **Searched answers are narrower.** When Claude searched, it named fewer brands, and the top three took a larger share. Some brands lost ground, as Mailchimp did.
- **Third-party comparison pages carry weight.** sequenzy.com, emailvendorselection.com and emailtooltester.com came back in most searched answers. How those pages describe your product shapes what Claude reads.
- **Your own site can surface.** brevo.com and sender.net were returned often. Clear, current pricing and comparison pages on your domain can match the queries Claude writes.
- **Dated content matches dated queries.** Many queries carried 2025. Keep yearly roundups and pricing pages current.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which email marketing brand did Claude name most often

Brevo. From memory it appeared in 76.7% of answers, and when told to search it appeared in 63.3%. That is a measure of what Claude said, not a judgment of which product is best.

### Did Claude search the web on its own for these questions

No. With search available but not required, Claude ran 0 searches across 60 answers. It searched only when the system prompt told it to, and then searched in 58 of 60 answers.

### Why did Mailchimp drop when Claude searched

The data shows the change, not the cause. From memory Mailchimp appeared in 53.3% of answers and was named first 15 times. When told to search it appeared in 30% and was never named first. Searched answers named fewer brands overall, about 5.6 per answer.

### Do these results match what people see in the Claude app

Not necessarily. The study used the Anthropic API at low effort with no current date. The Claude apps can behave differently, including in when they search.
