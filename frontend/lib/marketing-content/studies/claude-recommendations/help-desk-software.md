CiteLadder asked Claude Sonnet 5.5 20 buyer questions about help desk software. Each question ran three times in three conditions: from memory, with web search available, and told to search first. From memory, Claude named Freshdesk most often. When told to search, Help Scout moved to the top. This page reports what Claude said through the Anthropic API. It is not a ranking of which product is best.

## What Claude was asked

The 20 prompts covered five kinds of buyer intent. Some asked for an overall pick. Some described a specific buyer or use case. Others focused on budget, on a feature, or on a comparison shortlist.

Examples include:

- "What's the best help desk software right now?"
- "Best help desk software for a three-person support team at a startup?"
- "Best open source ticketing system I can self-host?"

Other use-case prompts covered an internal IT service desk, a HIPAA-compliant healthcare clinic, a school district and a managed service provider. The requests gave no current date. Another model listed the brands in each answer, and code did the counting.

## Brands Claude named from memory

With no tools, Claude gave 60 answers and named 78 distinct brands. It averaged 8.2 brands per answer. The top three brands took 25.3% of all mentions.

Five brands appeared in more than half of the answers:

- Freshdesk, in 72% of answers
- Zendesk, in 68%
- Help Scout, in 67%
- HubSpot, in 60%
- Intercom, in 55%

Zendesk was usually named early. It came first in 28 answers, with an average position of 1.9. Freshdesk appeared in more answers but came first in only 8.

The core list was stable across runs. Zendesk and Help Scout were each named in every run for 12 prompts. Freshdesk, HubSpot and Intercom were each named in every run for 11. Further down the list, Atlassian (35%), Zoho, Gorgias, Front and Zammad were less consistent. Overall run agreement from memory was 60.9%.

## How often Claude searched when it could

When web search was available but not required, Claude rarely used it. Only 4 of 60 answers involved a search, with 13 searches in total.

The results looked much like the memory condition. Zendesk appeared in 78% of answers and was named first in 27. HubSpot, Help Scout and Intercom followed. Freshdesk dropped to 48%. Answers named 92 distinct brands in this condition, averaging 9.4 per answer.

For most help desk questions, then, Claude answers from what it already knows unless it is told to search.

## What changed when Claude was told to search

When the system prompt told Claude to search first, all 60 answers used search. Answers got shorter on brands. They averaged 6.5 brands each and named 72 distinct brands in total. Run agreement fell to 46.7%, so the lists varied more between runs.

Four brands stayed in the top five: Freshdesk, Zendesk, Help Scout and HubSpot. The order changed. Help Scout was named most often, in 68% of answers. Zendesk appeared in 57%, though it was still named first most often, in 16 answers. Freshdesk appeared in 50% and HubSpot in 42%.

Intercom left the top five. It fell from 55% from memory to 22%. Zoho entered the top five, rising from 27% to 37%. Brands tied to IT service desks also showed up more, such as Freshservice (27%) and Jira Service Management (25%).

The brand extraction sometimes counted a parent company and its product separately. Freshworks and Freshdesk appear as two entries, and so do Zoho and Zoho Desk.

## What Claude searched for

Across the 60 searched answers, Claude ran 280 searches. 45.7% of queries contained the word "best". 39.9% contained a year. Of those, 111 named 2025 and 6 named 2026, even though the requests gave no date.

Many queries were comparison searches that named brands directly:

- "Zendesk AI agents vs Intercom Fin vs Freshdesk Freddy resolution rate pricing"
- "best alternatives to Zendesk Freshdesk ServiceNow help desk software 2025"
- "open source help desk software alternatives Zammad FreeScout UVdesk"

Because Claude often put brand names into its queries, the brands it already knew shaped what it found. None of the 60 searched answers included inline citations.

## Which sites Claude's searches returned

A few sites came back again and again. These counts show how many searched answers had each site in their results:

- featurebase.app, in 77% of searched answers
- desk365.io, in 65%
- getmacha.com, in 63%
- thecxlead.com, in 55%
- freshworks.com, in 45%

tidio.com, eesel.ai, bluetweak.com, zendesk.com and hiverhq.com also appeared in about 40% of searched answers. Most of these are vendor blogs and review sites that publish comparison lists. Only two vendor sites in this list, freshworks.com and zendesk.com, belong to brands that Claude named often.

## What a help desk brand can take from this

Memory matters most in this category. With search merely available, Claude searched in only 4 of 60 answers. A brand's place in Claude's existing knowledge decides most answers.

Search changes the shortlist when it happens. Help Scout and Zoho gained share when Claude was told to search. Intercom lost share. Search results come largely from third-party comparison pages, so being listed on sites like featurebase.app, desk365.io and getmacha.com affects what Claude finds.

Queries often carry a year and the word "best". Comparison content that is dated and kept current matches these queries more closely. Use-case pages matter too, since many prompts described a specific team, industry or setup.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which help desk brand did Claude name most often?

From memory, Claude named Freshdesk in 72% of answers, with Zendesk at 68% and Help Scout at 67%. When told to search, Help Scout was named most often, in 68% of answers. This reflects what Claude said, not which product is best.

### Does Claude search the web for help desk questions on its own?

Rarely. With search available but not required, only 4 of 60 answers involved a search, with 13 searches in total.

### Which sites did Claude's searches return most often?

featurebase.app appeared in 77% of searched answers, desk365.io in 65%, getmacha.com in 63% and thecxlead.com in 55%.

### Do these results match the Claude app?

Not necessarily. CiteLadder ran this study through the Anthropic API at low effort, with no current date in the requests. The Claude apps can behave differently.
