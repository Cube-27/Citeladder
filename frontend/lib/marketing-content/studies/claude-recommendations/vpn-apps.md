CiteLadder asked Claude Sonnet 5.5 20 buyer questions about VPN apps, three times each, in three conditions: from memory, with web search available, and told to search first. Proton VPN was named in the most answers in every condition. Telling Claude to search kept the same top five brands but changed their order and how often each was named first. This page reports what Claude said through the Anthropic API. It is not a ranking of which VPN is best, and the Claude apps can answer differently.

## What we asked Claude

We wrote 20 questions a VPN buyer might ask. They covered five intents: best overall, a specific buyer or use case, budget or free, feature-led, and comparison shortlists. Each question ran three times in each condition. The requests gave no current date.

Some examples, quoted exactly:

- "What's the most trustworthy VPN app right now?"
- "Best VPN for watching foreign streaming libraries while traveling abroad"
- "Which VPNs have independently audited no-logs policies?"

Other prompts asked about gaming, torrenting, journalists, censorship, a non-technical parent on an iPad, small businesses with remote staff, and students on a budget. Another model listed the brands in each answer, and code counted them.

## Brands Claude named from memory

With no tools, Claude gave 60 answers that named 44 distinct brands, about 5.2 per answer. The top three brands took 43.1% of all mentions.

- **Proton VPN** appeared in 93.3% of answers. It was named in every run for 17 prompts.
- **Mullvad** appeared in 66.7% of answers. It was named first 33 times, more than any other brand, with an average position of 1.9.
- **NordVPN** appeared in 63.3% of answers.
- **Surfshark** appeared in 46.7% of answers.
- **ExpressVPN** appeared in 43.3% of answers.

IVPN followed at 33.3%, then Windscribe and Private Internet Access at 16.7% each. Answers were fairly stable: run agreement across the three runs was 77.2%. So from memory, Proton VPN showed up almost everywhere, and Mullvad was the brand Claude most often put first.

## How often Claude searched when it could

When web search was available but not required, Claude searched in 7 of 60 answers, with 8 searches in total. Most answers still came from what the model already knew.

The results looked close to the memory condition. Proton VPN appeared in 88.3% of answers. NordVPN rose to 71.7% and was named first 10 times. Mullvad appeared in 60% and was still named first most often, 26 times. ExpressVPN appeared in 58.3% and Surfshark in 53.3%. CyberGhost entered the top ten at 10%.

## What changed when Claude was told to search

When the system prompt told Claude to search before answering, it searched in all 60 answers. The top five brands stayed the same as from memory: Proton VPN, Mullvad, NordVPN, Surfshark and ExpressVPN. No brand left or entered the top five. Proton VPN remained the brand named in the most answers.

The order inside the top five moved:

- **Proton VPN** appeared in 73.3% of answers, down from 93.3% from memory.
- **NordVPN** appeared in 71.7% and was named first 25 times, with an average position of 1.9. It became the brand Claude most often put first.
- **Surfshark** appeared in 56.7% and **ExpressVPN** in 53.3%.
- **Mullvad** fell to 36.7% of answers and was named first 8 times, down from 33 from memory.

Answers named fewer brands, 4.5 on average, and varied more between runs: run agreement dropped to 55%. IPVanish and CyberGhost appeared in the top ten at 8.3% each. Only 1 answer included inline citations.

## What Claude searched for

Told to search, Claude ran 263 searches. 45.7% of queries contained a year, and 45.7% contained the word "best". The year 2025 appeared in 113 queries and 2026 in 8. Since the requests gave no current date, the years reflect what the model assumed.

Sample queries, quoted exactly:

- "best VPN for streaming Netflix libraries abroad 2025"
- "VPN audits no-logs verified Mullvad Proton NordVPN ExpressVPN Surfshark PIA audit"
- "best business VPN small business remote employees 2025"

Many queries mirrored the prompt and added a year. Some named specific brands directly, which means brands Claude already knew shaped what it looked up.

## Which sites the searches returned

Review and comparison publishers dominated the results. Across the 60 searched answers:

- techradar.com and tomsguide.com each came back for 78.3% of answers.
- security.org came back for 73.3%.
- cyberinsider.com came back for 71.7%.
- gizmodo.com came back for 70%.
- cybernews.com came back for 65%.
- thebestvpn.com came back for 50%.

pcworld.com, comparitech.com and en.wikipedia.org followed. No VPN vendor site appears in the top ten hosts. When Claude searched, it read what third-party reviewers wrote about VPNs.

## What a VPN brand can take from this

From memory, Claude named a small set of brands often and consistently. Being in that set depends on what the model learned in training, which a brand cannot change quickly.

Search changes the order more than the membership. When told to search, NordVPN was named first far more often and Mullvad far less. The sources behind that shift were review sites such as techradar.com, tomsguide.com and security.org. A VPN brand that wants to appear in searched answers should check how it is covered on those sites, especially in pages for "best" lists with a current year.

Use-case prompts matter too. Queries targeted streaming, students, audits and small businesses. Coverage that ties a brand to a clear use case gives searches something specific to find. ## Frequently asked questions

### Which VPN did Claude name most often

Proton VPN, in all three conditions. It appeared in 93.3% of answers from memory, 88.3% with search available, and 73.3% when told to search. This counts mentions in Claude's answers and is not a judgment of which VPN is best.

### Does Claude search the web for VPN questions on its own

Rarely in this study. With search available but not required, Claude searched in 7 of 60 answers, with 8 searches in total.

### Why did Mullvad drop when Claude was told to search

From memory, Mullvad appeared in 66.7% of answers and was named first 33 times. When told to search, it appeared in 36.7% and was named first 8 times. The searches mostly returned review sites, and NordVPN was named first more often in those answers. The data shows the shift but does not show the exact cause.

### Will the Claude app give the same answers

Not necessarily. These results come from Claude Sonnet 5.5 through the Anthropic API at low effort, with no current date in the request. The Claude apps can use different settings and give different answers.
