## What do the five yardsticks measure?

Each yardstick counts a different thing, so name the unit before comparing numbers.

- **Referral clicks (StatCounter):** how many visits each chatbot sends to websites that run StatCounter's tracker. StatCounter describes its network as over 3 billion page views a month across over 1 million websites. Grok is excluded because it does not pass referral data in its header. ([StatCounter press release](https://gs.statcounter.com/press/google-gemini-overtakes-perplexity-to-become-second-largest-source-of-ai-chatbot-referrals-to-websites))[\[4\]](#source-4)
- **Prompt volume (Comscore):** each chatbot's share of AI prompts across Comscore's panel. The release does not state the panel size. ([Comscore](https://www.comscore.com/Insights/Press-Releases/2026/9/Comscores-Q2-2026-AI-Intelligence-Report))[\[8\]](#source-8)
- **Audience (Sensor Tower):** "True Audience" share, which Sensor Tower defines as unique users across mobile apps and web. It is a modeled estimate. ([Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-ai-2026))[\[9\]](#source-9)
- **Survey (Pew Research Center):** the share of US adults who say they ever use a given chatbot, from a survey of 5,119 adults weighted to represent the US adult population. ([Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/americans-and-ai-2026-chatbots-smart-devices-and-views-on-impact/))[\[11\]](#source-11)
- **Company-reported users:** each company picks its own unit and definition. OpenAI reports weekly users. Google reports monthly users. ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/scaling-ai-for-everyone/); [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/one-billion-monthly-users/))[\[12\]](#source-12)[\[13\]](#source-13)

A referral share shows who sends clicks to websites, a prompt share shows where people ask, and an audience share shows how many different people use a tool. None of them shows how often your brand appears in answers.

## Which chatbot sends the most referral clicks?

ChatGPT, by a wide margin in every month we read. In September 2026, StatCounter attributed 80.48% of worldwide chatbot referrals to ChatGPT, followed by Gemini (10.91%), Perplexity (5.85%), Microsoft Copilot (1.79%) and Claude (0.95%). ([StatCounter, September 2026 data as read on 20 October](https://gs.statcounter.com/ai-chatbot-market-share))[\[1\]](#source-1)

We do not know. StatCounter's press page lists no release that explains the October 2026 change. In the table we read on 8 November, ChatGPT has 51.58% and Gemini 28.86%, followed by Perplexity at 15.04%, Claude at 2.30%, Copilot at 2.18% and DeepSeek at 0.05%. ([StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/ai-chatbot-market-share), [press page](https://gs.statcounter.com/press))[\[1\]](#source-1)[\[3\]](#source-3)

Until StatCounter explains it, we do not compare October with earlier months and we quote the two months side by side, with the date.

### Does the US look different?

Yes. The US table for September 2026 had ChatGPT on 75.17% and Gemini on 13.26%, with Copilot at 6.64%, Perplexity at 3.02% and Claude at 1.81%. Copilot is stronger there than in the worldwide table. The US page available when we checked still showed September, so we have no October US figures. ([StatCounter, United States](https://gs.statcounter.com/ai-chatbot-market-share/all/united-states-of-america))[\[2\]](#source-2)

### Do referral shares move quickly?

Yes, even before October. StatCounter's April 2026 release put Claude at 2.91% for March 2026, more than double its February share. It also noted that Claude's weekly share peaked at 3.6% in mid-March before falling to 2.49% the next week, and its CEO suggested the jump may have been partly news-driven. ([StatCounter press release](https://gs.statcounter.com/press/google-gemini-overtakes-perplexity-to-become-second-largest-source-of-ai-chatbot-referrals-to-websites))[\[4\]](#source-4) In September, Claude was under 1%.

### How big are AI referrals next to search?

AI chatbots are still a small referral source. StatCounter's September 2026 comparison of referral sources put search at 91%, social at 8.61% and AI chatbots at 0.4%. In its head-to-head view, Google sent 99.61% of the referrals counted between Google and ChatGPT. ([StatCounter, search vs social vs AI](https://gs.statcounter.com/search-vs-social-vs-ai-chatbot-market-share); [Google vs ChatGPT](https://gs.statcounter.com/google-vs-chatgpt-market-share))[\[5\]](#source-5)[\[6\]](#source-6) StatCounter compares only the referral categories it lists, so these are not shares of all traffic. For more datasets on how much traffic AI sends, see our [AI referral traffic statistics](/blog/ai-referral-traffic-statistics-2026).

## How much of AI prompt volume does each chatbot have?

ChatGPT had half of prompt volume in Comscore's panel in June 2026, down from 70% in January, while Gemini and Claude gained. Comscore's Q2 2026 AI Intelligence Report, released on 22 September 2026, gives these shares. ([Comscore](https://www.comscore.com/Insights/Press-Releases/2026/9/Comscores-Q2-2026-AI-Intelligence-Report))[\[8\]](#source-8)

| Share of AI prompt volume | January 2026 | June 2026 | Change in points (our arithmetic) |
| ------------------------- | -----------: | --------: | --------------------------------: |
| ChatGPT                   |          70% |       50% |                           down 20 |
| Gemini                    |          17% |       30% |                             up 13 |
| Claude                    |           2% |       11% |                              up 9 |

The release also reports AI assistant reach: 29% of mobile users and 35% of desktop users visited one in June 2026, compared with 12% for mobile and 25% for desktop in January 2025.

**Our reading:** prompt volume is the closest yardstick here to "where do people ask?", and it shows a large shift in share across the first half of 2026. It comes from one panel, and the release does not state the panel's size, so treat the points as directional.

## How many people use each chatbot?

On audience and survey data the race is closer than on referrals. Sensor Tower's State of AI 2026 report says ChatGPT's True Audience share fell below 50% for the first time in March 2026. TechCrunch, reporting on the same report, gave end-of-May shares of 46.4% for ChatGPT, 27.7% for Gemini and 10.3% for Claude, and monthly user estimates of over 1.1 billion, 662 million and 245 million. ([Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-ai-2026); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/16/chatgpts-market-share-slips-below-50-for-first-time/))[\[9\]](#source-9)[\[10\]](#source-10) The full report is gated, so we have relied on Sensor Tower's public summary and TechCrunch's account of it.

Sensor Tower also reports that Claude's True Audience grew 452% year over year in May, and that its US share rose from 4.4% to nearly 14% over the same period. It links the shift away from a single dominant player partly to Claude's "rapid adoption among professional and high-intent user segments."[\[9\]](#source-9)

Pew's survey shows a similar order among US adults ([Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/americans-and-ai-2026-chatbots-smart-devices-and-views-on-impact/))[\[11\]](#source-11):

- 49% say they ever use AI chatbots, up from 33% in 2024 (Pew notes that the 2024 question was worded differently).
- ChatGPT 44%, Gemini 24%, Copilot 17%, Meta AI 14%, Grok 8%, Claude 6%.
- 42% say they use chatbots to search for information.
- 60% say they read AI summaries at the top of search engine results.

More US adults say they read AI summaries in search results than say they use any chatbot.

## How do Google AI Overviews and AI Mode fit in?

They sit outside most chatbot tables, and they carry the largest reported audience. At Google I/O on 19 May 2026, Sundar Pichai said AI Overviews has over 2.5 billion monthly active users and AI Mode has passed 1 billion. Google's Search post from the same day said AI Mode queries had been "more than doubling every quarter since launch." ([Google I/O keynote](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/sundar-pichai-io-2026/); [Google Search update](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/search-io-2026/))[\[15\]](#source-15)[\[16\]](#source-16)

In Alphabet's Q2 2026 remarks on 22 July, Pichai repeated that AI Mode has passed 1 billion monthly active users, said the Gemini app has 950 million, and said Google now sends "billions of clicks to websites every week through AI features in Search." He also said Google has combined AI Overviews and AI Mode into one Search experience. ([Alphabet Q2 2026 remarks](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/message-ceo/alphabet-earnings-q2-2026/))[\[14\]](#source-14) On 11 August, Google said the Gemini app had passed 1 billion monthly users. ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/one-billion-monthly-users/))[\[13\]](#source-13)

Comscore's panel gives an independent view of reach. It found that 39.4% of Google desktop searches in June 2026 showed an AI overview, up from 25.8% in July 2025, and that Copilot Search rose from 11.8% to 17.3% over the same period. ([Comscore](https://www.comscore.com/Insights/Press-Releases/2026/9/Comscores-Q2-2026-AI-Intelligence-Report))[\[8\]](#source-8) For more on how often AI Overviews appear, see our [AI Overviews statistics](/blog/google-ai-overviews-statistics-2026).

The user counts are company-reported, and the remarks do not define how a monthly active user is counted. We did not find an independent count of AI Overviews users; Pew's finding that 60% of US adults read AI summaries in search results is the closest independent measure we saw.

**Our reading:** a click from an AI Overview arrives from google.com, so referral-based datasets are likely to count it as search, not as an AI chatbot. StatCounter's FAQ describes treating clicks from Bing's chat feature as Bing search referrals in the same way. ([StatCounter FAQ](https://gs.statcounter.com/faq))[\[7\]](#source-7) That means a headline such as "ChatGPT has 80% of AI search" leaves out the AI answer surface with the largest reported audience.

## Why do AI market share numbers disagree?

The sources measure different things at different times, so most of the gaps below come from definitions. The clearest cases:

### Claude: sizeable in prompts and audience, small in clicks

Claude has 11% of prompt volume (Comscore, June) and 10.3% of the app and web audience (Sensor Tower, end of May), but 6% of US adults (Pew) and 0.95% of worldwide referral clicks in September (StatCounter). StatCounter's October table puts it at 2.30%, still well below its other shares. ([Comscore](https://www.comscore.com/Insights/Press-Releases/2026/9/Comscores-Q2-2026-AI-Intelligence-Report); [Sensor Tower via TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/16/chatgpts-market-share-slips-below-50-for-first-time/); [Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/americans-and-ai-2026-chatbots-smart-devices-and-views-on-impact/); [StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/ai-chatbot-market-share))[\[1\]](#source-1)[\[8\]](#source-8)[\[10\]](#source-10)[\[11\]](#source-11)

**Our reading:** we cannot tell from public data why the gap exists. Possible explanations include a usage mix weighted toward work tasks that need fewer outbound links, answers that link out less often, and the makeup of StatCounter's site network. None of these has been tested here.

### ChatGPT: half the prompts, most of the clicks

ChatGPT had 50% of prompt volume in June (Comscore) but 80.48% of referral clicks in September (StatCounter). The months differ, so the gap is indicative only. It suggests that clicks to websites are concentrated in fewer engines than prompts are.

### Perplexity: the month changes the answer

Perplexity's worldwide referral share was 5.85% in September and 15.04% in October. Given the unexplained October change, we would not describe it as a trend. ([StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/ai-chatbot-market-share))[\[1\]](#source-1)

### Gemini: company count versus panel estimate

Google reports over 1 billion monthly Gemini app users as of August. Sensor Tower estimated 662 million Gemini users across apps and web in its June report. ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/one-billion-monthly-users/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/16/chatgpts-market-share-slips-below-50-for-first-time/))[\[10\]](#source-10)[\[13\]](#source-13) Google's figure is a company count with no published definition, and Sensor Tower's is a modeled panel estimate from an earlier month. Both can be accurate for what they measure, so neither can stand in for the other.

### Weekly versus monthly, and the missing Anthropic number

OpenAI reported over 900 million weekly active ChatGPT users on 27 February 2026. ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/scaling-ai-for-everyone/))[\[12\]](#source-12) Weekly figures cannot be compared directly with Google's monthly figures.

We found no user count published by Anthropic in the sources we read. Every Claude user figure in this article is a third-party estimate.

### Assistants built into other products

Embedded assistants are hard to see in referral data, because they do not always send visitors through a recognizable chatbot referrer. Surveys capture them better, which may help explain why Meta AI (14%) and Copilot (17%) score as they do among US adults in Pew's survey. That is our interpretation, not a measured finding. ([Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/americans-and-ai-2026-chatbots-smart-devices-and-views-on-impact/))[\[11\]](#source-11)

## Which AI engines should you track?

Track ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Gemini first, then add others based on your own data. Market share shows where people are; only a separate check shows whether your brand appears in the answers they get. **Our reading** of the data above, as a starting point for a tracking set:

1. **ChatGPT:** first on every chatbot yardstick. Track it.
2. **Google AI Overviews:** the largest reported audience of any AI answer feature. If Google sends you meaningful traffic, track it, and consider AI Mode as it grows.
3. **Gemini:** second on referrals, prompt volume, audience and the survey.
4. **Claude:** third on prompt volume and audience but small on referrals. It matters most when your buyers are technical or professional users, the segment Sensor Tower credits for its growth.
5. **Perplexity and Copilot:** visible in referral data, and Copilot is stronger in the US. Add them if your own logs show them sending visitors.

Then check the yardstick that is specific to you. Your own [AI referral traffic](/platform/ai-referral-analytics), from analytics and server logs, shows which assistants actually send people to your site. Next, define a set of buyer-style prompts and run them on the engines you chose. ## Frequently asked questions

### What is ChatGPT's market share in 2026?

It depends on the measure and the month. StatCounter put ChatGPT at 80.48% of chatbot referral clicks in September 2026 and 51.58% in October, after a change in its series that it has not explained. Comscore had ChatGPT at 50% of AI prompt volume in June 2026, and Sensor Tower put its app and web audience share at 46.4% at the end of May. Always quote the measure and month with the number.

### Why did StatCounter's ChatGPT share fall in October?

We do not know. StatCounter's October 2026 table shows ChatGPT at 51.58%, down from 80.48% in September, while Perplexity rose from 5.85% to 15.04% and Claude from 0.95% to 2.30%. StatCounter's press page lists no release that explains the change, so we treat October as a break in the series and do not compare it with earlier months.

### Is Gemini catching up with ChatGPT?

Gemini has gained ground on prompt volume, rising from 17% to 30% of Comscore's panel between January and June 2026 while ChatGPT fell from 70% to 50%. ChatGPT is still ahead on every measure we read. Google also reports that the Gemini app passed 1 billion monthly users in August 2026.

### Is Claude overtaking Gemini?

Not on any measure we read. Comscore had Claude at 11% of prompt volume against Gemini's 30% in June 2026, and Sensor Tower put Claude at 10.3% of audience against Gemini's 27.7% at the end of May. Claude's share has grown quickly on both measures, though.

### How many people use Claude?

Anthropic has not published a user count that we could find. Sensor Tower estimated 245 million monthly users in its June 2026 report, and Pew found that 6% of US adults say they ever use Claude. These are estimates with different methods, so they should not be combined.

### Do Google AI Overviews count as AI search?

Yes, by most definitions, but chatbot market share tables usually leave them out. Google reports over 2.5 billion monthly active users for AI Overviews, more than any chatbot figure cited here, though the counting methods differ. Because AI Overviews sit inside Google Search, their clicks tend to show up as search traffic.

### What is Perplexity's market share?

StatCounter had Perplexity at 5.85% of worldwide chatbot referral clicks in September 2026 and 15.04% in October, but the October table follows an unexplained change in the series. In the US, its September share was 3.02%. The prompt-volume, audience and survey sources we cite do not report Perplexity separately.

### Which AI engines should I track?

Start with ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Gemini, which lead the public data. Add Claude if your buyers are technical or professional users. Then use your own referral data to decide whether Perplexity, Copilot or others deserve a place.
