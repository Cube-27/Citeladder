**Brand → Competitors** compares you with the competitors you track, using the same filters as [Insights](/insights/). **Edit competitors** opens **Agent → Context → Competitors**, where the tracked list lives.

## You and your competitors

The table lists you and each tracked competitor with **Visibility**, **Share of voice**, **Position**, **Win rate**, **Sentiment** and **Change**. Sentiment shows the net score with how many mentions were classified, and reads **Too few classified** below 20. Win rate reads **Not measured** for answers collected before it was recorded. When a list is capped, its caption says how many rows are shown out of the total, such as "10 of 32".

## A page for each competitor

Open a competitor's row to see its own page:

- **Key figures**: the competitor's visibility, share of voice, position, win rate and sentiment, each with yours beside it.
- **A trend against you**: their line and yours for the measure you pick, with the same markers as the Insights trend.
- **Sources when the competitor is named**: the domains cited in those answers, marked **You're cited too** when the domain is also cited in an answer naming you. Being cited together is co-occurrence; it does not show that the page mentions you.
- **Named instead of you**: the prompts whose answers named the competitor and not you, with their topic and on how many engines that happened. Open a prompt to see its page.

## Review suggested competitors

**Suggested competitors** lists businesses your answers point to that you do not track yet. A suggestion comes from a domain cited in answers or from a business name answers repeat; a name is suggested once it appears in at least three prompts across at least two engines in one audit. **Found by** reads **Cited domain** or **Named in answers**, and **Seen in** and **Engines** say how often. Names are found from the next audit after this was added; earlier audits are not re-read.

Suggestions never join your competitors until you choose **Accept**. **Reject** removes a suggestion, and later audits never suggest that domain or name again. Each suggestion is listed once, from its latest audit.
